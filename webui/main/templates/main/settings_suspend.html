Suspend

Hosts on this list are suspended over SSH once they have been idle longer than their timeout (no requests, no active connections, no blocking checks). The watchers wait for /symbios after a reboot, so a late disk unlock never breaks them. A host never suspends itself; /tmp/no-suspend (non-empty) blocks a suspend on both sides. SSH key access to each target is required once (ssh-copy-id root@<host> as root on this host).
