How it works
  • Every night at 00:05 a complete snapshot of your data (/symbios) is created.
  • Databases (Nextcloud etc.) and the user directory are exported first, so snapshots are always consistent.
  • Unchanged files are stored only once (hard links), so snapshots need far less space than a full copy every day.
  • With a backup server, everything is mirrored there; with encryption enabled as an unreadable archive.
Restoring

Below under "Your backups" you can bring back any previous state: either just a single service (e.g. Nextcloud from yesterday) or the whole system. Files AND databases are restored automatically.
