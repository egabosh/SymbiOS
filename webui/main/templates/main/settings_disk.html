How it works

By default, your data lives on the same disk as the operating system. Moving it to a separate disk gives you more space and better performance.

  1. Plug in or connect a new disk
  2. Select it from the list above
  3. Optionally enable encryption
  4. Click "Move Data"
  5. Your data is copied automatically
Important: Back up your data first. The target disk will be completely erased (Move Data only; Adopt never formats).
